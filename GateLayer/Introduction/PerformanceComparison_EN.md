# Performance Comparison

Compared with other L2 solutions on Ethereum, like Optimism Mainnet, X Layer, and opBNB, Gate Layer has a lower gas fee and a higher block gas limit, which means the gas fee will be more stable when the traffic on Layer 2 increases.
